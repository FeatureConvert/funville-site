Funville Privacy Policy

Funville: Idle Adventure Park · Last updated October 10, 2026

Short version: Funville has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't collect personal information. If you say yes when the game asks, it sends us anonymous error reports and, from version 1.1, anonymous play stats, so we can fix problems and see what to improve. If you choose to join the rankings, your park's name and scores are shown to other players. You can change either any time.

What we collect

No personal information. Funville has no sign-up, no ad networks, no third-party analytics and no tracking. We don't know who plays, and we never see your saved park. Everything the game sends us is anonymous. The rankings are optional and use only a park name you choose (see below).

Anonymous error reports and play stats (only if you say yes)

On your second visit the game asks whether it may send them ("Help improve Funville?" from version 1.1, "Help us fix bugs?" before). Only if you say yes does it send us a short report when something goes wrong inside the game, at most once a day a summary when it has been running slowly, and, from version 1.1, a few play stats.

A report contains the game's version (and its engine's), your device's model (for example "iPhone17,1") and its iOS version, the game's graphics setting, how long the game had been open, and what went wrong inside the game. A slowdown summary has frame-rate figures instead, which screen was showing and how busy it was (for example how many guests were on it), and whether battery saver was on. Reports have no name, no account, no device or advertising identifier and nothing from your saved park, so they can't be linked to you. Before a report is sent, the game blanks out anything in it that looks like an email address, an identifier or a long number.

Play stats are daily counts, for example: that the game was opened that day, how many minutes it was played, how far the first-time guide got, which attractions and blocks were built, how many gems were earned and what they were spent on, whether the Weekly Cup, Putt of the Day or Weekly Open was played, the graphics setting, and which in-app item was bought (the item only: nothing about you or your payment). Each day's counts go with the game's version, that it's the iOS version, the date the game was first opened on that device (so we can compare new and long-time players), and the group of any test you're in (see below). They have no name, account, device or advertising identifier and nothing from your saved park. Our server adds them into daily totals and keeps only those totals, with nothing that says whose they were.

Reports and play stats are stored with Cloudflare, which runs our server. Reports are deleted after 90 days and play-stat totals after 400 days. Your IP address is used only to limit how many requests can arrive in a minute and is never stored, and we keep no request logs. You can turn them on or off at any time: More, then Settings, then "Error reports & play stats" ("Send error reports" before version 1.1). Saying no, or turning them off, stops both and deletes anything still waiting to be sent.

Trying out changes (from version 1.1)

Now and then we may try a change on some players before deciding whether to keep it (for example a different length for a Boost). Your game picks its group itself, with a random number it keeps on your device and never sends. If you said yes to play stats, they include the group's name (for example "boost_hours:b") so we can compare the groups' totals. Nothing else about the test is sent.

Community goals (from version 1.1)

Sometimes every park works towards a shared goal, for example a number of hole-in-ones by Sunday. While one is on, your game sends our server how many it has added (only the goal, the number and the game's version) and asks for everyone's total. The server keeps only each goal's running total; nothing about who added what is sent or kept. Because it's only a number, it doesn't need your yes to reports. Your saved park remembers which goals it helped with, so it can give you the reward.

Send feedback (from version 1.1)

Settings has a "Send feedback" button. If you use it, what you type goes to our server with the kind of message you picked (a bug, an idea or something else), the game's version, that it's the iOS version and your device's model (for example "iPhone17,1"). No name, account or identifier is added, so please don't type personal information. If you'd like a reply, use "Contact us" to email us instead (see below). Feedback is stored with Cloudflare and deleted after 365 days; your IP address is used only to limit how many messages can arrive and is never stored.

Event dates and game settings

When the game starts, and when you come back to it after a few hours, it downloads a small settings file from our server (for example the dates of seasonal events and, from version 1.1, game numbers such as prices and timings, and any community goal or test that's running), so we can adjust them without an app update. That request only says which version of the game it is and that it's the iOS version. Nothing about you is sent or kept.

The game also asks our server the time now and then (when it starts, when you come back to it and every few minutes while it's open), so a device clock set ahead can't earn extra. That request carries nothing about you either.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, which handles the connection to deliver it (see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy).

Rankings (only if you join)

World Rankings and the Weekly Cup's standings are optional. To join, you choose a name for your park. Please don't use your real name or anything personal in it. Everyone who plays can see your park's name, its place, its all-time earnings and Owner Level, and its Weekly Cup score.

When you join, our server gives your park a random player number, which is shown with the rankings, and a secret code that stays with you: your device keeps it, and so does your park's iCloud copy if you use Game Center, so your other devices can use it too. The server stores a scrambled version of it, not the code itself. The server keeps the park name and whether it has been checked; your all-time earnings and Owner Level; your Weekly Cup score for each of the last 12 weeks; when you joined, last renamed and last sent a score; and any reports of park names you made or received. It doesn't keep your real name, an account, a Game Center or device identifier, or your IP address. Scores are sent with the time on your device's clock, which the server uses only to turn away scores from a clock set far ahead; it isn't kept.

Park names are checked before they're shown: by our own word list, and by an automatic safety check that runs on Cloudflare (Workers AI). Players can report a name. A reported name may be hidden until we look at it, and we may hide or block names that break the rules.

You can rename your park once a day. To leave, open World Rankings and tap "Leave the rankings": everything above is deleted from our server straight away. If a park sends no scores for 400 days, it is deleted too, and Weekly Cup scores older than 12 weeks are deleted every day.

Your saved park

Your park is saved on your device. If you're signed in to Game Center, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud (Game Center saved games) so you can pick up on a new device. That copy holds your park, your device's model name (for example "iPad8,1") and, if you've joined the rankings, your player number and secret code, so your other devices carry on under the same name. It's stored by Apple in your iCloud account; we can't see or access it. You can delete it from your iCloud storage in your device's Settings.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, achievements and leaderboard scores are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Funville only sends Game Center your in-game progress (for example your Owner Level, your Weekly Cup score and, from version 1.1, your Putt of the Day and Weekly Open scores). From version 1.1, the Weekly Open's scorecard also shows that week's standings from Game Center (players' Game Center names and scores, as Apple shares them with the game). They're shown on your device and never sent to us.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The game only learns which item you bought so it can give it to you.

Notifications

If you allow them, Funville schedules reminders on your device (for example when your park's away earnings are full). They're created on the device itself; nothing is sent to us.

If you email us

If you write to us, we'll have your email address and whatever you tell us, and we use them only to reply. The "Contact us" button in Settings fills in the game's version, your device type and its iOS version so you don't have to. Ask any time and we'll delete your emails.

Children

Funville is made for everyone, including families. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Error reports, play stats and community goals are anonymous and contain nothing about the player, and feedback carries only what's typed into it. The rankings are optional; a park name should never include a real name, and names are checked before they're shown. Funville is rated 4+ and has no chat or messaging. If a child has put a real name on the rankings, "Leave the rankings" removes it at once, or email us and we'll delete it.
