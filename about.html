About

Made by one person

Funville is made by Nexrax, a one-person studio.

The story

Funville began as a nine-hole course and grew a block at a time. The rule from the first day: no ads, ever.

How it was made

Funville is built in the Godot 4 engine by one person. Everyone whose art, music and sound effects are in the game is credited in the game itself: open Settings, then Credits.
