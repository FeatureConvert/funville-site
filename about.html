About

Made by one person

Funville is made by Nexrax, a one-person studio.

The story

Funville began as a nine-hole course and grew a block at a time. The rule from the first day: no ads, ever.

How it was made

Funville is built in the Godot 4 engine. Its art comes from CC0 and royalty-free asset kits, the code was built with AI coding assistance, and park names are checked by an AI safety service.
