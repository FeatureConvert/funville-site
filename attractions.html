Attractions

Every hole, every block

Start with nine mini golf holes. Then buy the blocks across the street and down the side streets, twenty-six in all, each a new venue with rides of its own. The newest blocks open on later laps of your Franchise.

The course

Nine hand-shaped mini golf holes with windmills, water and bridges. Build them one by one, level them up and give each one a Marshal.

  • Hole 1 Welcome Bend
  • Hole 2 The S-Curve
  • Hole 3 Lily Pad Green
  • Hole 4 The Old Windmill
  • Hole 5 Rock Garden Split
  • Hole 6 Cave Run
  • Hole 7 Stream Bridge
  • Hole 8 Castle Keep
  • Hole 9 Maple Falls

Glow Putt

A blacklight hall with nine more holes: neon rails, glowing balls, a loop-the-loop, a glowing windmill and a cup on a low bridge. Each one is built, leveled, staffed and played like the course outside, day or night.

  • Glow Hole 1 Neon Mile
  • Glow Hole 2 Loop the Loop
  • Glow Hole 3 Diamond Post Green
  • Glow Hole 4 Stardust Tunnel
  • Glow Hole 5 Windmill Sails
  • Glow Hole 6 Sky Bridge
  • Glow Hole 7 Castle of Stars
  • Glow Hole 8 Double Dip
  • Glow Hole 9 Grand Loop Finale

Across the street

The first blocks, each on a lot across the street from the course.

Block Desk

Petting Farm

Pigs, sheep, llamas, cows and pony rides, and a little steam train. Every pen you open lets its animals out.

Attractions
Pig Pen, Sheep Meadow, Llama Lane, Moo Barn, Pony Rides, Farm Express

Arcade

Machines pay tickets, and the Prize Counter swaps tickets for plush prizes. A Ticket Frenzy now and then makes every machine pay more.

Attractions
Retro Cabinets, Hoop Shot, Air Hockey, Claw Machine, Pinball, Dance Machine, Pixel Racers, Mega Claw, Space Blasters, Golden Claw

Block Desk

Pond

Dig a pond: paddle and bumper boats, sailboats, a fishing pier, a pirate ship and a splashy log flume.

Attractions
Fishing Pier, Paddle Boats, Bumper Boats, Sailboats, Pirate Ship, Log Flume

Block Desk

Dino Land

A palm jungle with raptors, a triceratops, a stegosaurus, a T-Rex and a brontosaurus. Every paddock you open brings its dinosaurs out.

Attractions
Raptor Run, Triceratops Trail, Stego Grove, T-Rex Kingdom, Bronto Valley

Block Desk

Go-Karts

A go-kart track. Every kart class you open puts more racers on the track.

Attractions
Mini Karts, Go-Karts, Pro Karts, Night Racing, Grand Prix

Block Desk

Haunted Graveyard

A lantern walk, a skeleton crypt, zombies, a vampire and a ghost tower. It comes alive after dark, when every attraction earns more.

Attractions
Lantern Walk, Skeleton Crypt, Zombie Patch, Vampire Manor, Ghost Tower

Block Desk

Fun Yard

Bumper Cars, a Drop Tower you can time for a Perfect Drop, a High Striker whose bell rings now and then, and a Bounce Barn that's busier with families about.

Attractions
Bumper Cars, Drop Tower, High Striker, Bounce Barn

Block Desk

Thrill Coaster

A steel roller coaster. Every coaster you open sends out another train, and quick dispatches by hand build a Thrill Streak.

Attractions
Junior Coaster, Wild Mouse, Steel Thunder, Loop Legend, Sky Screamer

Block Desk

Space Port

Moon craters, a rover, an alien and a star-gazing dish. Speeder racers run laps, a real rocket blasts off, and a mission rocket flies Space Missions for souvenirs.

Attractions
Moon Rover, Alien Encounter, Star Gazer, Speeder Racers, Rocket Launch

Block Desk

Castle Kingdom

A hedge maze, a trebuchet, a jousting tournament, a wizard's tower and Ember, the friendly dragon who loops the courtyard.

Attractions
Hedge Maze, Trebuchet Toss, Jousting Tournament, Wizard's Tower, Dragon's Keep

Block Desk

Adventure Camp

An Adventure Camp in the pines: a campfire sing-along, an archery range, canoes on the creek, and a treehouse fort with a rope bridge and a zip line.

Attractions
Campfire Circle, Archery Range, Canoe Creek, Treehouse Fort, Zip Line

Big Top & Little Rides

A striped circus tent with a show every few minutes, a ticket booth, flying elephants and a midway. The crowd gathers, then the show runs.

Attractions
Ticket Booth, Flying Elephants, Big Top Ring, Midway Games, Popcorn Wagon

Later lapsBlock Desk

Safari Tram

Ride a little open tram round a savanna and a jungle, past a watering hole, as animals wander up to the track. Tap each one into your Safari Album: 16 animals to spot.

Attractions
Tram Depot, Watering Hole, Jungle Trail, Photo Hide, Ranger Lodge

Later lapsBlock Desk

Harbor

A fishing pier, rowing boats, fish and chips and a lighthouse. Cargo ships tie up at the dock: tap one to unload it.

Fishing Pier, Boat Rentals, Cargo Dock, Fish & Chips, Lighthouse

Later lapsBlock Desk

Petting Zoo

Attractions
Goat Pen, Bunny Hutch, Feed Stand, Alpaca Walk, Pony Rides
