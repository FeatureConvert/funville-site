A mini golf park that grows with you

Start small. Every round your golfers play earns a little money, and that money builds the park.

  1. Nine holes to startNine hand-shaped mini golf holes, with windmills, water and bridges. Tap a hole to start a round.
  2. Blocks across the streetBuy the lots across the street for new venues: a Petting Farm, an Arcade, a Pond, Go-Karts, Dino Land and many more.
  3. A town around your parkThe empty land past your blocks grows into the town of Funville, with a water tower that says FUNVILLE.
  4. Franchise to new townsWhen your park is grown, move to a new town for Stars that stay with you, and build again.

Tap the park

Everything happens in the park itself. Tap a hole to start a round and tap a ride to run it. Hire Marshals and staff, and your park keeps earning while you're away.

Twenty-three blocks have a Block Desk at the gate, where one tap runs every ride that is waiting. Press and hold a Marshal to give them a name.

How the park works

Putt it yourself

Putt of the Day is a new hole every day, the same for everyone. Touch anywhere, pull back and let go.

The Weekly Open is nine holes a week, one round that counts, with medals and a scorecard to share.

The ball really rolls: it bounces off the walls, climbs the ramps and lips out when it's hit too hard.

Watch it come alive

Families wander the paths and line up for rides, and a hole-in-one gets a cheer. Day turns to golden dusk and lantern-lit night, and the Haunted Graveyard comes alive after dark.

Passing rain showers pop the umbrellas, then a rainbow comes out. Critters drop by while you're away.

No ads, ever.

Funville is free and fair. Nothing stops the game to ask you to watch a video.

  • The free Boost charges as you play. No video needed.
  • No loot boxes, no spins, no random paid items.
  • No pop-up offers. Everything in the Shop is there whenever you want it.
  • The VIP Pass is a one-time purchase, never a subscription.
  • The Weekly Cup is real players only: no bots.
  • No account needed.
