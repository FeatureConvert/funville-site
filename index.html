Funville

Funville: Idle Adventure Park · a cozy idle tycoon with no ads, ever.

A little mini golf course grows, one block at a time, into a whole adventure park.

Start with nine hand-shaped holes. What your golfers earn builds the park: a petting farm, an arcade, go-karts, Dino Land, a haunted graveyard, a steel roller coaster and a Space Port where the rocket really lifts off. Families wander the paths and cheer hole-in-ones, and your staff keep the park earning while you're away. There are no ads, ever, and nothing stops the game to ask you to watch a video.
