Funville

Funville: Idle Adventure Park · a cozy idle tycoon with no ads, ever.

A little mini golf course grows, one block at a time, into a whole adventure park, and it keeps growing for weeks.

Start with nine hand-shaped holes. What your golfers earn buys the blocks across the street: a Petting Farm, an Arcade, go-karts, Dino Land, a Haunted Graveyard that comes alive after dark, a Space Port where the rocket really lifts off, and many more down the road, all the way to a Harbor where cargo ships sail in. Your staff keep the park earning while you're away. There are no ads, ever, and nothing stops the game to ask you to watch a video.

What's inside

  • Nine mini golf holes with windmills, water and bridges, and a Glow Putt hall with nine more
  • Putt of the Day to play by hand, and the Weekly Open to try for the best score of the week
  • Blocks to buy and run, from bumper cars and a drop tower to a chairlift Sky Glider and a Safari Tram
  • Franchise to a new town when your park is done, and start again bigger
