Park names and fair play

Funville: Idle Adventure Park · World Rankings and the Weekly Cup

The rankings are optional. If you join, you pick a name for your park and other players can see it. Here is how we keep it kind.

  1. What is not allowed. Names that are unkind, hateful, sexual or violent; real names; email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details; and advertising.
  2. How names are checked. Against our own word list and an automatic AI safety check (Cloudflare Workers AI) before a name is shown. If the automatic check can't finish, the name is held back (others see "Park #...") and checked again automatically later.
  3. Anyone can flag a name. A flagged name may be hidden until a person looks at it.
  4. We look within 2 days. Reports are read by a person, not only by software.
  5. Questions or appeals. Email Nexrax@icloud.com. A name that was hidden by mistake can be restored.

Report a name

Open World Rankings in the game and tap the flag next to the name. Or email Nexrax@icloud.com with the name and, if you can, the rank it showed. Funville has no chat or messaging, so nobody can contact you through a park name.

If your own park name was reported

You'll see it shown as "Park #..." with a note in the rankings, and you can choose a new name (once a day). The same happens if the automatic check hides a name. If you think it was a mistake, email us and we'll take another look.

Leave the rankings

Open World Rankings and tap Leave the rankings at the bottom. Your park name and scores leave the live rankings at once. Backup copies expire within 14 to 30 days. The privacy policy has the detail.

Under 13

Funville is family friendly and not directed at children under 13. If a note sent to us suggests that the player is under 13 (for example "I am 9"), we delete it and don't keep it. Please don't put a real name in a park name; if one is there, leave the rankings or email us and we'll remove it.
