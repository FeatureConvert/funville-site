In one paragraph

Funville is a cozy idle tycoon where a little mini golf course grows into a whole adventure park: a petting farm, an arcade, go-karts, Dino Land, a haunted graveyard, a steel roller coaster and a Space Port where the rocket really lifts off. Families wander the paths, cheer hole-in-ones and line up for rides, and your staff keep the park earning while you're away. There are no ads, ever, and nothing stops the game to ask you to watch a video.

In three paragraphs

Funville starts with nine hand-shaped mini golf holes, all windmills, water and bridges. Every round your golfers play earns a little money, and that money builds the park: upgrade the holes, hire Marshals to run them, add a snack bar, benches and a fountain, then buy the blocks across the street. Each one opens a new venue, from a Petting Farm and an Arcade with a Prize Counter full of plushies to a Pond with a log flume, Dino Land, a Thrill Coaster, a Castle Kingdom with a friendly dragon and a Space Port.

It's built to be watched as much as played. Day turns to golden dusk and lantern-lit night, rain showers send guests under umbrellas and leave a rainbow behind, critters drop by while you're away, and the Haunted Graveyard comes alive after dark. When the park is grown, Franchise to a new town (Sunny Shores, Snowy Peaks, Canyon Springs) for permanent Stars, and spend them in the Star Workshop.

Funville is free and fair: no ads, no loot boxes, no pop-ups asking you to pay. The free Boost doubles your earnings without a video. An optional VIP Pass, a one-time Welcome Pack and gem packs are there for players who want to support the game.

Coming in the first update

Putt of the Day, a new hole every day that you putt by hand; the Weekly Open, nine holes a week with medals and a scorecard to share; a ball that really rolls, bounces and lips out; Glow Putt, nine more holes under blacklight; a town that grows around your park; and fifteen new blocks, from a skate park and an aquarium to a Dodgeball Dome and Laser Tag.

About the developer

Funville is made by one person, Nexrax, in the Godot 4 engine, with 3D art from free (CC0) kits by Kenney, Quaternius and others. It began as a nine-hole course and grew a block at a time. The rule from the first day: no ads, ever.
