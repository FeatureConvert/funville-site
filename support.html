Funville Support

Funville: Idle Adventure Park · for iPhone and iPad

Need a hand? Email Nexrax@icloud.com. We reply within 2 days. It helps to mention your device, your iOS version and what you were doing. In the game, Settings > Contact us / Report a bug fills most of that in for you.

Lost purchases and refunds

I bought something but don't have it.

Open the Shop, go to the Store tab and tap Restore Purchases. The VIP Pass and the Welcome Pack's Golden Putter come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Gem packs are used up when bought, so they can't be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds, so we can't refund a purchase ourselves. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account that bought it, choose the purchase and tap Request a refund. If something went wrong in the game, email us too and we'll make it right in the game where we can.

Moving your park

Sign in to Game Center with the same Apple Account on both devices. Funville keeps a copy of your park in your iCloud and offers to load it when it finds one that's further along.

Delete my data

Your park lives on your device; delete the app to remove it. The rest is small, and the privacy policy has the full detail:

  • Rankings: open World Rankings and tap Leave the rankings. Your park name and scores leave the live rankings straight away. Backup copies expire within 14 to 30 days.
  • Anonymous reports and stats: turn them off in Settings. They contain nothing about you, so there is nothing to look up.
  • Your iCloud copy: delete it from iCloud storage in your device's Settings. We can't see or remove it.
  • Anything else, or you'd like it confirmed: email Nexrax@icloud.com and we'll delete it.

Report a park name

Park names are checked automatically before they're shown. If you see one that isn't kind, open World Rankings and tap the flag next to it. A flagged name may be hidden until we look at it. You can also email Nexrax@icloud.com with the name. Read the park-name rules for what is not allowed and how to get a name restored.

Common questions

Does my park earn while I'm away?

Yes, once you've hired Marshals. They earn for a while even when the app is closed; the limit grows as you build holes and open blocks across the street, and the Star Workshop can stretch it further.

What is the Weekly Cup?

A little pop-up park each week (Monday to Sunday) with a twist. Earn money there to win Bronze, Silver and Gold medals for gems. VIP and other bonuses don't count in the Cup, so it's fair for everyone.
